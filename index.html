The platform

Why restaurants choose Orderlya

Own your repeat customers

Delivery aggregators rent you their audience and take 20–30% of every order — and the customer stays theirs, not yours. Orderlya flips that: a flat monthly subscription with zero commission on any sale, while every customer, phone number and order history lives in your own WhatsApp and dashboard. Your regulars come back to you directly, and repeat orders cost you nothing extra.

Native operations, not a bot bolted on

This isn't a chat widget stuck onto your website. A live kitchen display, delivery dispatch with driver tracking, your own branded ordering page (hosted on Orderlya), a POS register, menus, loyalty, cash-on-delivery and reporting all ship in the base — one system that actually runs the restaurant instead of just catching messages.

Conversational AI in Arabic and English

Your customer orders the way they already talk — a WhatsApp message in Egyptian Arabic or English — and the AI understands it, builds the order, confirms it, and only pulls you in when something needs a human. No app to download, no form to fill: the lowest-friction way a MENA restaurant can take an order, open 24/7.

The AI front desk

Your AI runs the front desk. You step in only when it matters.

Orderlya answers every message the second it lands, takes the order, and confirms it — no missed WhatsApp at the 9pm rush. When something needs a human touch, it hands the conversation to you, then takes it back when you're done.

  • Takes orders in Egyptian Arabic or English, around the clock.
  • Knows your live menu — won't sell what's sold out or quote a wrong price.
  • Escalates the tricky ones — a complaint, a special request — straight to your inbox.

The AI never invents a dish or promises a delivery time it can't keep — anything it's unsure of, a person sees. That's the point of takeover.

What platform can I use to take my restaurant's orders on WhatsApp?

Orderlya is an AI-powered, WhatsApp-first ordering platform for MENA restaurants — an AI agent takes and confirms your customers' orders inside WhatsApp in Arabic or English, your kitchen sees them instantly, and delivery runs itself. 14-day free trial, no credit card.

How long does setup take?
