Both are cloud POS — here's the difference in focus

Sapaad is a cloud restaurant POS used across the region. Orderlya is a full cloud POS too — touch register, kitchen display, dine-in tables, cash control, reporting — but it's built around the thing most POS systems bolt on afterwards: taking orders directly from customers on WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger with an Arabic-speaking AI. If ordering is where your growth is, that changes which tool fits.

WhatsApp AI ordering is native, not a bolt-on module

In Orderlya a customer messages your WhatsApp, an AI takes and confirms the order in Egyptian Arabic or English, and it lands on the same kitchen board and POS as a walk-in sale — one system, no separate ordering app to reconcile. Orders on your own channels carry no per-order commission. That's the core of the product, not an add-on you pay extra to switch on.

Priced for an independent kitchen, and live the same day

Orderlya is a flat monthly EGP subscription that sits below the legacy POS tiers, with zero per-order commission on your own channel — no per-terminal licensing and no enterprise contract to sign. And you can be live the same day: connect WhatsApp, load your menu from a photo, and start taking orders on a real shift. See the pricing page for exactly what each plan includes.

Arabic-first, in EGP, cash-on-delivery normal

Orderlya's interface and its customer-facing AI both work in natural Arabic, pricing is in Egyptian pounds, and cash on delivery is a first-class flow — built for how restaurants in Egypt and the MENA region actually operate, not translated after the fact. Every order arrives on your own channel, so the customer's number, name and history stay in your dashboard — they are yours to keep.

Everything a growing kitchen needs, in one plan

Beyond the register: a full Kitchen Display System, dine-in floor and QR-pay tables, driver dispatch and COD reconciliation, inventory with recipe costing and AI invoice scanning, loyalty and gift cards, multi-branch Brand HQ roll-ups, VAT reporting (with ETA e-invoicing in preview), and analytics with demand forecasting. It's a flat monthly EGP subscription — try the whole thing free for 14 days, no credit card.
